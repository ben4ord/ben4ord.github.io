Recommendations Based on Your Needs:

If you want ultra low cost, easy setup, and enough for under 100 weekly visitors: Hostinger is a top pick.

If you're hosting a static site with no backend at all: Netlify or GitHub Pages give you free hosting and SSL, no fuss.

If you want something affordable but reliable from the developer community: Consider Ethernet Servers or Shock Hosting.

If you're planning to use WordPress and want easy scalability later: Bluehost gets you started simply, or spring for SiteGround for better performance long-term.
