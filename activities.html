Park Activities

Arts & Crafts

Join us for arts and craft at the Palisades RV Park. Sewing, knitting, painting, and more!

Concerts and Dancing

Palisade RV Park offers occasional concerts and line dancing. Contact your host or the office to get more details on dates.

Holiday Activities

Summer Holidays are celebrated here at the Palisade RV Park! Join us for BBQs, music and fun.

Sunday Worship

Come worship with us every Sunday. All are welcome!

Weekly Brunch

Catch up with neighbors and friends at our weekly weekend brunch.

Jeep Outings

Have a off roader you're looking to put to use? Palisade RV Park offers jeep outings at all levels of adventure. Contact the front office for scheduled events.

Cards & Board Games

Take a step away from technology and enjoy some fun group games. Ranging from traditional cards games to puzzles and more.

Exercise Group

Come exercise with us!

The Mission of the Gunnison Home Association (GHA) is to assist in the provision of basic needs and amenities to seniors in the Gunnison Valley. These services may include housing, food assistance, transportation, medical needs or social and cultural enrichment. It is our goal through our activities and grant program to provide financial assistance to be used to enhance the lives of senior citizens in our area, whether full-time or seasonal residents.
